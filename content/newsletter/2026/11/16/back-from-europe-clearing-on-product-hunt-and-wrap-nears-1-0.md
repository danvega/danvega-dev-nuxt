Happy Monday and welcome to another edition of the newsletter.

It's been a couple of weeks since my last one. I just got back from Europe. Clearing had a big day. And Wrap is getting close to 1.0. I also have two new videos and two new Spring Office Hours episodes for you. Let's get into it.

## Back from Europe

Last week I was in Europe for two conferences.

First was Porto. Nate Schutta and I gave the opening keynote at [Porto Tech Hub](https://portotechhub.com/conference-2026/): Fundamentals of Software Engineering in the Age of AI. It was my first time in Portugal, and it was an amazing experience.

Nate and I wrote a book on this topic. We also teach 8-hour workshops on it. So cutting it down to 50 minutes is always the hard part. But I think we nailed the message: the fundamentals haven't changed in the age of AI.

So many people came up to thank us afterward. They said it was just what they needed to hear right now. That was really special. Thank you to Porto Tech Hub for having us.

![Dan Vega and Nate Schutta at Porto Tech Hub](/images/newsletter/2026/11/16/porto-tech-hub-selfie.jpg)

![Opening keynote at Porto Tech Hub](/images/newsletter/2026/11/16/porto-tech-hub-keynote.jpg)

Then I went to [J-Fall](https://jfall.nl) in the Netherlands for my talk, Spring Cleaning: What Boot 4 Lets You Delete. The night before, there was a wonderful speaker dinner where I made some new friends.

J-Fall started with a few early sessions. Then came the keynote in a massive theater. I've seen photos of talks in that room for years. I always told myself that one day I would be up there. Then I looked at the schedule and realized my talk was in that same room. I was following the great [Dr. Venkat Subramaniam](https://agiledeveloper.com/aboutus.html), and Adam Bien was up after me. Presenting in that room was such a cool experience. I got some great feedback after the talk, too.

![The keynote theater at J-Fall](/images/newsletter/2026/11/16/j-fall-theater.jpg)

In between conferences, I spent some time at the JetBrains office in Amsterdam. It was a lot of fun hanging out with friends. I even recorded a podcast with [Anton Arhipov](https://www.jetbrains.com/guide/authors/aa/). I'll let you know when it comes out.

![The podcast studio at the JetBrains office in Amsterdam](/images/newsletter/2026/11/16/jetbrains-podcast-studio.jpg)

All in all, it was an amazing trip. One thing really stuck with me: how well attended conferences are in Europe. I love the sense of community there, and how much people value being together. I hope we see more of that over here.

If you came to either talk and said hi, thank you. That's my favorite part of these trips.

## Clearing Is on Product Hunt

On Friday, Clearing was featured on Product Hunt. Clearing is the Mac planner I built to get everything out of my head and into one place. I got some good feedback after the launch that some of you might want to use it on iPad as well. I fixed some issues with the iPad layouts, and now you can get Clearing for iPhone, iPad and Mac for one price.

Seeing it there was a great feeling. If you haven't tried it yet, now is a good time.

[Visit clearingplanner.com](https://clearingplanner.com)

## Wrap Is Getting Close to 1.0

Wrap is the Mac screen recorder I've been building. It knows what you did while you recorded, not just what you said. It's getting closer and closer to 1.0.

That's thanks to some wonderful beta testers. They have sent me bug reports, ideas and honest feedback. Every one has made Wrap better. Thank you. It was also really awesome to see the [first video](https://x.com/wimdeblauwe/status/2120227454728184290) that was recorded, edited and published with Wrap that wasn't by me. Thanks, Wim!

## New Video: Every App Should Have an MCP Server

APIs are built for other programs. MCP is built for agents. If your app has an API, it should probably have an MCP server too. Then tools like Claude can use your app directly.

## New Video: Spring AI 2.1 and the Responses API

With newer OpenAI models like GPT-6 Astra, tool calling can break on the Chat Completions API. The fix is one property that switches Spring AI 2.1 over to the Responses API.
